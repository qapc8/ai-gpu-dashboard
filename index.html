How to read these prices
ON-DEMAND pay per hour, no commitment, stop any time COMMITTED · 1 YR you pay for every hour of the term, used or not SPOT interruptible, can be reclaimed CLUSTER multi-node reservation (16+ GPUs, InfiniBand), a different product RESERVED ONLY no on-demand plan to compare with
Each discount compares a commitment with the same provider's on-demand rate. Break-even utilisation is the share of hours the GPUs must be busy for the commitment to cost less than paying on demand for the hours actually used. Only top-tier GPUs are shown; providers that quote commitments only through sales (CoreWeave, Nebius, Crusoe, Oracle, RunPod, Vultr) are shown as "—".
